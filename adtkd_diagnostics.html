ADTKD Diagnostics & Consensus Guidelines

Evidence-based clinical recommendations, quality control benchmarks, and diagnostic decision pathways for MUC1 variable number tandem repeat (VNTR) mutation detection in chronic kidney disease, formulated through European ADTKD-Net consortium consensus.

Overview & Genetic Concepts

Autosomal Dominant Tubulointerstitial Kidney Disease (ADTKD) is an inherited progressive nephropathy characterized by slowly progressive loss of renal function, bland urinary sediment with minimal or absent proteinuria/hematuria, and unremarkable renal ultrasound early in the disease course. Patients frequently present with early hyperuricemia and gout, ultimately progressing to end-stage kidney disease (ESKD) between the third and seventh decades of life.

The genetic etiology comprises several genes, primarily UMOD (encoding uromodulin), HNF1B, REN, and SEC61A1. Pathogenic variants in these genes are readily identified using standard short-read exome or targeted panel sequencing (NGS). In contrast, ADTKD-MUC1 represents a major diagnostic challenge due to the unique genomic architecture of the MUC1 locus on chromosome 1q22.

Exon 2 of MUC1 contains a Variable Number of Tandem Repeats (VNTR) composed of 20 to 125 nearly identical 60-basepair (20-amino acid) repeat units with exceptionally high GC content (~82%). Standard short-read sequencing reads (~150 bp) cannot span multiple identical repeat units, causing standard read mappers and variant callers (e.g. GATK, DeepVariant) to misalign reads or fail to identify repeat-embedded insertions.

The prototypic pathogenic variant is a single cytosine insertion within a homopolymeric poly-C tract (commonly referred to as 59dupC or position 67 of the 60-bp repeat unit). This +1 frameshift produces a toxic truncated neoprotein, MUC1-fs, which misfolds, accumulates within the endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi apparatus of renal tubular epithelial cells, and triggers chronic proteotoxic stress, tubular cell death, and interstitial fibrosis.

The Atypical Non-dupC Frameshift Spectrum: Notably, international multicenter screening by the ADTKD-Net consortium revealed that approximately 16% of molecularly confirmed ADTKD-MUC1 cases carry non-canonical frameshift variants at diverse positions within the VNTR unit rather than the canonical 59dupC. These non-dupC frameshifts are completely invisible to 59dupC-targeted probe-extension assays (such as SNaPshot minisequencing) or mass spectrometry. Consequently, variant-agnostic computational short-read screening and full VNTR-resolving technologies are essential to prevent false-negative diagnoses.

Consensus Diagnostic Workflow

Through consensus within the European ADTKD-Net consortium (EJP RD JTC 2023), an expert-informed two-pathway diagnostic workflow was established to optimize diagnostic yield, minimize turnaround time, and integrate computational Short-Read Genotyping into clinical practice.

  • Pathway 1: Secondary Screening in Unexplained CKD (CKDx): Patients undergoing standard diagnostic panel or exome sequencing for chronic kidney disease of unexplained etiology can be screened retrospectively or prospectively using VNtyper 2 directly on existing BAM, CRAM, or FASTQ alignments. This adds zero wet-lab cost and yields a molecular diagnosis in ~1.2% of unselected CKD cohorts and up to 5.7% of young-onset CKDx cases.
  • Pathway 2: Targeted High-Suspicion Evaluation: In patients presenting with a strong family history of dominant kidney disease, bland urine sediment, early gout, or biopsy-suggestive tubulointerstitial fibrosis, panel or exome sequencing with VNtyper 2 analysis serves as the primary computational diagnostic step. If VNtyper 2 is negative despite high clinical suspicion, evaluation must not end; dedicated orthogonal testing (long-read sequencing or SNaPshot) should be initiated.

Interpretation Criteria & Actionable Tiers

To ensure clinical safety and diagnostic precision, VNtyper 2 reports categorize results into three structured interpretation tiers based on alternate-read depth, depth score, cross-caller concordance, and regional coverage quality:

1. High Precision (HP / HP*)

High Confidence Reportable

Criteria: High variant depth score, robust alternate-read k-mer support, caller concordance, and unflagged regional coverage (mean depth ≥100× and ≤50% uncovered bases).

Clinical Action: In clinically concordant patients, supports direct diagnostic reporting in accordance with accredited laboratory policy. Orthogonal confirmation is optional, and primarily recommended for atypical non-dupC frameshifts, living kidney donor evaluations, reproductive decision-making, or when required by local accreditation frameworks.

2. Uncertain / Low Confidence

Confirmation Required

Criteria: Low Precision (LP) designation, novel or atypical non-dupC frameshift candidates, discordance between Kestrel and adVNTR callers, or suboptimal coverage metrics.

Clinical Action: Results should not be reported as definitive. Orthogonal confirmatory testing is mandatory before returning clinical findings. Long-read sequencing (PacBio SMRT or Oxford Nanopore) is the preferred confirmatory modality for resolving complex or non-dupC alleles.

3. Negative (No Variant Detected)

Context Dependent

Criteria: Absence of detectable alternate k-mers or HMM-supported frameshifts.

Clinical Action: If regional VNTR coverage is adequate (mean depth ≥100×) and pre-test suspicion is low, ADTKD-MUC1 is excluded with high confidence. However, if strong clinical or familial suspicion exists (dominant CKD with bland sediment, early gout) or if coverage is suboptimal (<100×), the negative short-read call is non-definitive; direct long-read sequencing or SNaPshot minisequencing should be pursued.

Diagnostic Modalities Comparison

Each diagnostic method possesses distinct strengths, variant coverage spectra, and operational requirements in clinical genetics:

VNtyper 2 / VNtyper Online

Short-Read NGS Zero Wet-Lab Cost Dual Variant Scope

Variant Spectrum: Detects canonical 59dupC AND atypical non-dupC frameshift variants.

Input Material: Standard short-read exome, genome, or panel alignments (BAM / CRAM) or raw FASTQ via SHARK k-mer filtration.

Turnaround Time: Under 3 minutes per sample (client-side or pipeline integrated).

Clinical Utility: Ideal first-line computational screening for all unexplained CKD cohorts and high-throughput diagnostic bioinformatic pipelines.

SNaPshot Minisequencing

Targeted Assay 59dupC Specific Only

Variant Spectrum: Canonical 59dupC single-base insertion ONLY (~84% of cases).

Input Material: Purified genomic DNA; locus-specific PCR followed by single-base primer extension and capillary electrophoresis.

Turnaround Time: 1 to 2 weeks.

Clinical Utility: Rapid, established orthogonal confirmation for canonical 59dupC. Limitation: Inherently blind to ~16% of patients harboring atypical non-dupC frameshifts.

Long-Read Sequencing (PacBio / ONT)

VNTR-Resolving Gold Standard Confirmation

Variant Spectrum: Complete VNTR structural assembly; detects all frameshift types (59dupC + non-dupC) and resolves repeat copy number.

Input Material: High-molecular-weight (HMW) genomic DNA; targeted long-range PCR or long-read capture.

Turnaround Time: 2 to 4 weeks.

Clinical Utility: Definitive gold standard for atypical frameshifts, discordant calls, novel alleles, and genotype-phenotype repeat-length characterization.

MUC1-fs Immunodetection

Neoprotein Biomarker Phenotypic Evidence

Variant Spectrum: Detects mutant MUC1-fs protein resulting from any (+1) frameshift within the VNTR.

Input Material: Formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded (FFPE) kidney biopsy tissue or exfoliated tubular cells isolated from fresh urine.

Turnaround Time: 1 to 3 days.

Clinical Utility: Confirms intracellular neoprotein accumulation when genetic results are ambiguous, sequencing data is unavailable, or non-invasive urinary cell analysis is desired.

Sequencing Quality Control & Exome Capture Recommendations

Short-read detection accuracy in repetitive loci depends heavily on the capture library preparation design and sequencing depth. The European ADTKD-Net consortium evaluated multiple capture kits:

  • Twist Exome v2 & IDT xGen Exome v1/v2 (Recommended): Provide reliable, robust read representation across the MUC1 VNTR locus via probe overlap and adjacent spillover sequencing. Downsampling experiments confirm stable variant detection down to approximately 80× regional mean coverage.
  • Agilent SureSelect v8 (Caveat): As a research exome design omitting repeat-region probes, it exhibits a distinct coverage gap across the MUC1 VNTR. Negative screening results obtained with this design are non-informative and cannot exclude ADTKD-MUC1.
  • Minimum Quality Thresholds: For diagnostic utility, sequencing datasets should demonstrate a mean regional VNTR depth ≥100× and no more than 50% uncovered bases. Samples falling below these thresholds should be flagged for repeat sequencing or direct orthogonal testing.
