Consent Notice (GDPR Art. 9)

Data controller: Dr. Bernt Popp, Berlin Institute of Health at Charité / Universitätsmedizin Berlin (support@vntyper.org).

What is sent: one small record built in your browser from two files in your result ZIP (coverage_summary.tsv and kestrel_result.tsv): the VNtyper version, the coverage QC status, binned coverage depths, whether VNtyper reported an unflagged call or a flagged call, its confidence class, a binned depth score, a coarse variant class (dupC or other), and the answers you select below. You see the exact record before it is sent.

What is not sent: no files, no sequencing reads, no sample names, no job or run IDs, no dates and no free text. The ZIP never leaves your computer. The record contains no identifiers and cannot be linked to an upload or an analysis job.

No withdrawal: because nothing in a donation identifies it, a donation cannot be found, changed or withdrawn once it has been sent.
