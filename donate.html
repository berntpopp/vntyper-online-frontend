GDPR Article 9 Informed Consent Notice

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR Art. 9), genetic and health information requires explicit, informed consent. Data controller: Dr. Bernt Popp, Berlin Institute of Health at Charité / Universitätsmedizin Berlin (support@vntyper.org).

What is donated: Only anonymous pipeline summary statistics (decision-bearing metrics, coverage depth, target kit, sequencing platform, and coarse phenotype tags). Raw genomic reads (BAM, CRAM, FASTQ) are strictly excluded and will never be accepted or stored. All sample identifiers are irreversibly hashed client-side before submission.
